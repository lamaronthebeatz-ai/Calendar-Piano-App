Piano samples used by src/features/theory/audio.ts

Source: Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Ogg Vorbis edition), recorded from a Yamaha C5 by Alexander Holm.
https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), as stated in the
archive's README ("Author: Alexander Holm").

Changes: notes C, D#, F#, A from C2 to C7 (file name = MIDI number), velocity layers v6 (soft) and v13 (loud);
trimmed to 4.2 s with a 1 s fade, mixed to mono, encoded as Opus (.ogg) and AAC (.m4a). Pitch is corrected at
playback to equal temperament at A4 = 440 Hz (cents measured from each recording, table RETUNE in audio.ts).
